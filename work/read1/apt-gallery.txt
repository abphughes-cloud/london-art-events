 About us
 Art in Perpetuity Trust was founded in 1995 by a group of artists in an old warehouse in South East London. At its freehold premises on the banks of Deptford Creek, APT provides 42 secure studios for visual artists, a contemporary art gallery, a working sculpture yard and an external public performance space.
