 MAGIC SYSTEM [https://autoitaliasoutheast.org/projects/magic-system/] 
 27 Nov 2026 — 28 Feb 2027
 Auto Italia presents MAGIC SYSTEM, the first UK solo exhibition by interdisciplinary Gambian-Swedish artist SUUTOO. Bringing together newly commissioned sound, sculpture and moving image, the exhibition explores image-making as myth, propaganda and fetish. Through an assembly of props, portals and noise, SUUTOO stages an abandoned play, continuing the artist’s inquiry into myth-making. MAGIC SYSTEM gathers strategies developed across a practice spanning performance, visual art, music and underground club culture.
