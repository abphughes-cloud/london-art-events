 Since 1988, Autograph has been a vital home for photography that explores race, representation, human rights and social justice – deepening understanding of ourselves and others.
 Autograph is a charity that ensures these stories are seen and celebrated, shaping a more inclusive future in the arts and beyond.

 Exhibitions On Now
 We believe that visual representation is a powerful force for change. Autograph creates – and contributes to – groundbreaking exhibitions, sharing the work of artists who use photography and film to highlight issues of race, identity, representation, human rights and social justice. We’ve been doing this since 1988.

 Accessibility
 Autograph is a fully accessible space with a step-free entrance at street level and a lift to all floors.
 Assistance dogs are welcome.
 Each floor has accessible bathrooms.
 A dedicated parking bay is available with advance booking.
 Large print captions are available at reception.
 If you would like to discuss your visit to Autograph, please contact us at 020 7749 1240 or info@autograph-abp.co.uk [mailto:info@autograph-abp.co.uk] .
 General Information
 Our exhibitions are free and unticketed, you do not have to book in advance.
 Photography for personal use is encouraged – without use of professional equipment.
 For the protection of artworks we ask that no food or drink is brought into the galleries. We welcome assistance dogs, however cannot allow other dogs into the building. Free lockers are located at reception.
 We welcome prams and pushchairs. Baby
