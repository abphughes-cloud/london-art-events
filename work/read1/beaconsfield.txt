Beaconsfield EarthAIR Autumn: 24 September-24 October 2026 
 Palestinian-Canadian artist Jumana Emil Abboud’s multidisciplinary practice explores cultural memory, lore, and entanglement. As Beaconsfield’s Autumn EarthAIR she researches the North Lambeth water table, its bearing on local climate resilience and the lived stories that might emerge.

 RACHEL GARFIELD 
10 November 2021 – 29 January 2022 
 The Struggle is a trilogy of three video works commissioned by Beaconsfield and developed with a range of partners. The three films, The Straggle (2012), Opening Up (2015) and A Glimpse (2019) are exhibited together for the first time, exploring the impact of familial interactions on the formation of subjectivity.
