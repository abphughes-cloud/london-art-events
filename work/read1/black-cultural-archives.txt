 From an activist vision to record and preserve our history, the Black Cultural Archives has grown into an internationally recognised heritage centre. We teach Black history, our collections reflect the experiences of Black people in the UK, and we proudly celebrate our heritage.
 ARCHIVES 
 The Archive collection promotes the importance of historical reflection as a powerful tool to help society learn from the past and work towards a more racially just and equitable future for all.

 Visiting the Black Cultural Archives
 Black Cultural Archives welcomes bookings for learning workshops, ordering research materials in our reading room and library, and attending events we’re hosting. We aren’t open for walk-ins yet, but we do open at select times for open days. Please check our events page for more information and sign up using our newsletter.
