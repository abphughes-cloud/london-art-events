 Public Events 
 The Art School hosts a variety of annual events, from student shows, celebrating the excellent work of our current Foundation, BA and MA graduates, to events that are part of wider contemporary art and craft festivals such as London Craft Week and The Big Draw.
 You are warmly invited to visit all our events and shows, and take the opportunity to see behind the scenes of the Art School.
 Join our mailing list [https://www.cityandguildsartschool.ac.uk/mailinglist/] to receive invitations to our public events and shows.
