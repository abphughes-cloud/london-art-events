 Deptford X Artist Weekender 2026 
 We are excited to announce our 2026 Weekender will be during the first weekend of October, hosted by our partner venues, Cockpit & Laban, two exceptional art institutions in Deptford. Over the 3 days we will present new work by artists who are based in or are connected to Lewisham. 
 Read More [https://deptfordx.org/home/dx-artist-weekender-2026/] 
 Deptford X Festival July 2025 
 This 26th edition of Deptford X free festival took on a bold new biennial format, expanding the festival to 18 days of incredible art from the 11-27 July 2025. We included three major strands of our programme with over 500 participating artists in 100+ artist-led Fringe projects, eight new artist commissions and a participatory street parade created with schools of Lewisham. 
 Explore the Programme [https://deptfordx.org/deptford-x-2025/]

 DX Artist Weekender 2026: Social
 Preview 2 October, Exhibition 3 & 4 October
 Over the 3 days we will present new work by artists who are based in or are connected to Lewisham.  The works include painting, collage, drawing, artists film, film and sculpture installations, photography, textile and performance.  Throughout the weekend we will also be hosting family workshops, artist workshops and film screenings.
 Exhibiting Artists – Khushboo, april forrest lin 林森, Ray Malone, Paula Morison, Elizabeth Oniri, Harald Smykla and Sam Wootton.
 Screening Programme Artists – James Jordan Johnson, Ursula Pelczar, Dhiren Shingadia, Ray Malone, Charlotte Young in collaboration with Phoebe Rayner.
