 hARTslane
is an art gallery 
 with a focus on community and participation.
 This is a place where artists and local people come together to share ideas, explore contemporary issues and be inspired.
We are committed to the value of art as a way to bring people together and closer to themselves. 
 About Us [https://www.hartslane.org/index.php/about/]
