 WHO WE ARE
 Shaping Artists Since 1845
 For more than 180 years, Heatherleys has championed traditional fine art skills and education. We are one of Britain’s longest-established independent art schools, and we are among the few art colleges that specialise in portraiture, figurative painting, printmaking, and sculpture. Courses are designed for all skill levels and needs, from professional diplomas to flexible evening, weekend, and short courses. Courses are open to students aged 19 years and over.
