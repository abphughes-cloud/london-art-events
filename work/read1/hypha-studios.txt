### PAGE https://hyphastudios.com/pages/events
Events
A Hypha space is not just about the display of artwork, but also meaningful community engagement with culture. In return for the free space, artists organise workshops, talks, and events for the entire local community. This brings people to the venue, engages a wide range of people, and brings the artistic projects to life.
