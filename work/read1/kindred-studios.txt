 WORKSHOP FROM HAND TO FONT
 18 NOVEMBER
14:00 16:30
Fully booked
Education Room
Kindred artist and designer Jihae Choe will guide you through the process of turning your own hand-drawn letters into a working digital typeface. 
 Your drawings will then be transformed into digital letterforms that can be typed with directly. Using the typefaces created during the workshop, you will experiment with words, spacing and composition, exploring the shift from hand-made marks to digital typography.
 To bring everything together, the group will collaboratively create a zine using the fonts made during the session. The zine will be designed and printed on site, and each participant will leave with a copy of the finished publication.
 Cost: £5 materials contribution, bring cash or bank transfer on the day.

 INTRODUCTION TO SCREENPRINTING
 BI-MONTHLY (more dates coming soon)
Screenprinting workshop
3 hours / booking essential 
 This fast paced hands-on workshop takes you through the full screen-printing process. From degreasing, coating a screen with emulsion, to exposing your image from an acetate and pulling your first prints. Create single colour prints and see why it has been a long standing favourited process by artists and agitators alike. No experience needed. Come with a willingness to get inky. Leave with a print, practical know how and the itch to do it all over again.
 Booking link [https://kindredprinthaus.setmore.com/] for kindred artists only
 SONIC SKETCHING WITH ABBAS ZAHEDI
 THURSDAY 26 NOV /21 JAN
15:00 - 16:00/ RSVP at community@kindredstudios.co.uk
Project Space
A monthly, hour-long session, led by influential award winning artist and lecturer, Abbas Zahedi, [https://abbzah.com/] where listening becomes drawing. 
 Please send any of your own field recordings, 24h before session to mixtape@abbzah.com [mailto:mixtape@abbzah.com] : a phone voice memo is perfect, nothing more technical needed. Abbas will combine the recordings into a single piece which you will listen to together and all sketch in response to what you hear. No experience in drawing or in sound needed, it's a deeper way into the embodied listening already happening across Kindred, letting noise, composition and drawing blur into one another. Part of Abbas' Stanley Picker Fellowship, and picking up where the last Kindred Support Group left off.
 CERAMICS MASTERCLASSE-DEMO: Colouring Clay: Beyond Glaze

Link to booking sent by email - Fully booked
Ceramics Workshop
 Award-winning international artist Manos Kalamenios [https://www.madebymanos.com/] will lead a masterclass exploring colour as an intrinsic part of clay, rather than a surface afterthought.
