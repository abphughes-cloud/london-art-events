 Discover our history
 Over the centuries, we’ve been a home, a school, a hospital - even a raging fire! We've hosted the great, the good and the glamorous, and survived all sorts of threats and dangers. 
 Find out more [https://www.lauderdalehouse.org.uk/about-us/our-history]
