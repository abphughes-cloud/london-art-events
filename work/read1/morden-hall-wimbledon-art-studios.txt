 Join London’s biggest artistic community 
 Wimbledon Art Studios is one of London’s largest art studio complexes: 350+ artists, designers, makers and creative businesses working under one roof in Earlsfield, SW17. 
 We rent affordable, professional studio spaces to creatives at every stage, from first studio to growing business. Choose a shared or individual studio across four sections, from £150 a month, on a monthly rolling contract with 24/7 access, 365 days a year.
 Painters, ceramicists, sculptors, photographers, textile artists, jewellers and more work here side by side. Their studios come with free Wi-Fi and parking, a career development programme, and the chance to sell their work at Wimbledon Art Fair twice a year.

 Studio rental FAQs
 How much does a studio cost? 
 Shared studios start from £150 a month and individual studios from £250, depending on the section, size and light, and subject to contract or availability. On top of rent there’s a refundable deposit (one month’s rent).
 How long do I have to commit for? 
 The minimum rental period is one month. After that, your studio runs on a monthly rolling contract, so there’s no long lease, and you have 24/7 access, 365 days a year.
 Can I see a studio before I decide? 
 Yes. Viewings are by appointment, Monday to Friday, 9am to 4pm. Send an enquiry [https://www.wimbledonartstudios.co.uk/enquiries] or call 020 8947 1183 [tel:02089471183] and we’ll arrange a time.
 What if there’s nothing available right now? 
 You can join our waiting list for £50, and we’ll contact you as soon as a matching studio comes up. If you go on to rent with us, the £50 comes off your deposit. If you no longer need the list, £45 is refunded.
