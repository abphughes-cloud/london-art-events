 A Journey Through Time and Culture [https://peckhamplatform.com/all-resources/resource-a-journey-through-time-and-culture/] 
 This free activity pack poses two questions: "What is a diaspora?" and "How do culture and creativity affect each other?" to develop a collection of cards for conversation around themes of diaspora and cultural identity. 
 Download the resource now [https://peckhamplatform.com/all-resources/resource-a-journey-through-time-and-culture/] 
 Our vision is a world where art builds community power. Where we collectively imagine new futures around the issues we care about. 
 To get there we bring artists and communities together, opening up space for those who are often unheard to respond to what’s happening in the world around us with bold, playful possibilities for our shared future. Our work is intergenerational, but with a special focus on young people.
