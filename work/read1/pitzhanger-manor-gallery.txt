 EXHIBITION CHANGEOVER: 11–22 NOVEMBER
 The main gallery will be closed for exhibition changeover from 11–22 November. Pitzhanger Manor remains open, although some rooms may be temporarily unavailable.
 Pay What You Can admission is available to all visitors who would normally pay for entry during this period.
 We look forward to welcoming you back from 25 November for Peter Blake: In the Studio [https://www.pitzhanger.org.uk/whatson/peter-blake/] .
