 Who We Are
 Sonsoles Print Studio was established in 2012 in Peckham by Sonsoles (Soni) Marquez. Run by a small team of professionally trained artist-printmakers, passionate and dedicated to building a creative community. We warmly welcome everyone to be part of our printmaking family. 
 Throughout the year we hold member exhibitions, open-studio events, one-off masterclasses, industry professional talks, as well as hosting workshops for a wide range of professional and community groups and teaching many beginners through our courses and workshops.
