 SLG Forever [https://www.southlondongallery.org/support-the-slg/slg-forever/] 
 The South London Gallery has been free and open to all for 135 years. In 2026 we have launched the SLG Forever anniversary campaign, to raise much-needed funds to build on this legacy and make an even greater impact.
 What's On [https://www.southlondongallery.org/whats-on/] 
 We host a wide range of events from artist talks, film screenings and performances to supper clubs, zine fairs and family friendly workshops. 
 Support Us [https://www.southlondongallery.org/support-the-slg/] 
 We need your support to keep the gallery and its activities free and realise our ambition for contemporary art to be accessible to all. All donations, large or small, make a genuine difference.
 Shop [https://www.southlondongallery.org/shop/] 
 Shop our curated selection of books, magazines, artists’ editions, stationery and gifts in-store and online. The bookshop is committed to highlighting independent publishers and championing reading across all ages.
 Schools [https://www.southlondongallery.org/learning/schools/] 
 The SLG's schools programme is free and open to schools and colleges across all key stages, as well as early years settings. Visit with your class and explore our programme.
