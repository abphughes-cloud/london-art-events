 Vanessa Hogge [https://vanessahogge.com/] 
 Thursday 1Oth December, 6.3Opm 
 Location: Copeland Park [https://www.thekilnrooms.com/location] 
 Free, open to all 
 Working in porcelain, Vanessa Hogge crafts ornate wallflowers and vessels. Each petal is sculpted by hand, bringing the clay to life in organic, decorative forms. No two flowers are identical, making each impressively textured piece unique. Vanessa has exhibited at Ceramic Art London, COLLECT and more. 
 find out more about our events 
 The Kiln Rooms is more than just a space to create; it’s a place to develop as an artist and connect with like-minded creatives. We host a programme of social events, bi-annual members’ sales, and 6 to 7 artist talks each year – which are open to everyone and free to attend. These events form part of our wider development programme [https://www.thekilnrooms.com/?page_id=12209&preview=true] , which members and staff have full access to – offering additional opportunities to grow their practice through group tutorials, technical support sessions, participation in sales, and more tailored events!
 Members Sales [https://www.thekilnrooms.com/open-studios] 
 Our bi-annual sales showcase the work of 80–100 emerging artists and designers working with ceramics across our Bellenden Road and Copeland Park studios, offering a chance to see handmade work, meet the makers, and visit the studios where the pieces are made.
 Check out our upcoming sales here! [https://www.thekilnrooms.com/open-studios?preview_id=3404&preview_nonce=053e7f899f&preview=true] 
 Artist talks 
 We provide a wide range of talks and demonstrations from invited guests covering all aspects of professional practice. Running roughly 6-7 talks per year, all are free to attend and everyone is welcome!
 The talks take place at our Copeland Park studio’s members area.
 social events 
 We say it a lot, but our community makes The Kiln Rooms. We keep it thriving through a number of social events to get involved in throughout the year, from pottery pub quizzes to drinks in the local pub and more informal get-togethers.
