 Heights 
 We’ve just activated one of the final floors at KOPPEL Heights, offering a new wave of studio spaces for artists, designers, and makers. Situated in the heart of Elephant & Castle, Heights is home to a growing creative community with bright, flexible spaces that adapt to a wide range of practices, from painting and sculpture to fashion, photography, and design!

 Hire our Chalk Farm Gallery!
 Collective 
 Hire our Chalk Farm gallery at 157 Regent’s Park Road, a flexible two floor space designed for exhibitions, launches, screenings, and cultural events. With over 1,100 sq ft, street facing windows, high ceilings, and adaptable project space below, the venue supports ambitious and experimental presentations within Camden’s creative community. Income generated through venue hire directly contributes to The Koppel Project’s wider charitable programme, helping sustain affordable studios, residencies, and public facing arts initiatives across London.
