 Become a supporter and help sustain our ambitious and innovative programme of artists commissions, local projects, talks and events. 
 The Showroom invests in the commissioning and production of new work. For over 40 years, The Showroom has been a pioneer of socially-engaged contemporary art practice, known for challenging what art can be and do. We champion emerging and underrepresented artists and practices, enable risk-taking and experimentation, and often present artists in London for the first time. 
 Individual giving provides essential support to The Showroom. Our Supporters Scheme offers different levels of support for those who want to become involved: Director’s Circe, Patrons and Friends.
